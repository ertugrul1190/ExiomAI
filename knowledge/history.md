# HISTORY, MIGRATION AND VISION

## From Equilibria (XEQ) to EXIOM (XEQM)

XEQM began as a Proof-of-Work coin on the legacy Equilibria (XEQ) chain and has more than seven years of continuous history. The Horizon upgrade moved it to pure Proof-of-Stake, gave it a verifiable supply and predictable emissions, and removed friction from running service nodes (no renewals, no 28-day lockouts). The coin became XEQM and the network and platform became EXIOM, built by XEQMLabs. EXIOM mainnet ("Ragnarok") launched May 6, 2026. Equilibria/XEQ is now sunset.

Sources: [EXIOM AI Instructions](https://xeqmlabs.com/ai-instructions), [The XEQMLabs Vision](https://xeqmlabs.com/blog/xeqmlabs-vision)

## The XEQ legacy swap (completed)

COMPLETED AND CLOSED. Holders sent legacy XEQ from their own wallet (never from an exchange, since exchanges can't make transaction proofs) to the official swap address, generated a transaction proof and submitted it on the swap portal. The window opened at block 1,781,094 (Mar 10, 2026, 12:00 PM ET) and closed at block 1,806,294 (~Apr 15, 2026): 35 days, 25,200 blocks. Late submissions were not accepted. Every deposit went into a public ledger with a SHA-256 fingerprint; the new chain was minted once to match the verified total, 276,917,604 XEQM; the spend keys of the deposit, genesis and distribution wallets were published so anyone can audit deposit to payout. Unswapped XEQ stays on the inactive legacy chain. XEQMLabs offers this as the EXIOM Coin Swap product for other projects' migrations.

Sources: [XEQMLabs Docs](https://xeqmlabs.gitbook.io/docs), [EXIOM Tokenomics Whitepaper](https://github.com/XEQMLabs/whitepaper)

## Mission and principles

XEQMLabs builds infrastructure for privacy-native apps: a private blockchain, a developer platform and a data oracle network, so privacy is the default rather than an afterthought. Principles: no trust required (every claim independently verifiable, from swap ledger to supply curve); privacy by default (balances, amounts and transaction graphs unreadable); build in the open (the same API for XEQMLabs and everyone else); sustainable by design (developer activity, not inflation, as the long-term funding engine). Privacy belongs in whistleblowing, healthcare eligibility, voting, credentials and data marketplaces, not only payments.

Source: [XEQMLabs Docs](https://xeqmlabs.gitbook.io/docs)

## Vision: a privacy platform, not just a coin

XEQMLabs' vision: make privacy programmable. "The coin is the fuel. The platform is the product. The ecosystem is the vision." Developers call API primitives for identity and access, data and intelligence, communications and publishing, governance and coordination, and commerce. Usage fees reward operators and fund development, so governance emissions can fall as revenue grows: a flywheel of more apps, more usage, more operators. XEQMLabs calls this a design principle, not a promise. Programmable privacy means verifying a condition without exposing the data behind it, delivering signed feeds without revealing who asks, and moving value without linking payer and payee.

Sources: [The XEQMLabs Vision](https://xeqmlabs.com/blog/xeqmlabs-vision), [What Is XEQM?](https://xeqmlabs.com/blog/what-is-xeqm)

## Regulatory context

The platform whitepaper notes the SEC's proposed Regulation Crypto Assets (published Aug 21, 2026: a startup exemption up to $5M over four years, a fundraising exemption up to $75M per 12 months, a conditional safe harbor) and the CLARITY Act market-structure bill in Congress. Both are proposals still in process, and neither is regulatory approval of EXIOM or XEQM. XEQMLabs calls XEQM a utility coin, not an investment product; its classification varies by jurisdiction; nothing promises price, returns or listings.

Source: [EXIOM Platform Whitepaper](https://xeqmlabs.com/blog/exiom-platform-whitepaper)
